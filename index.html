Common questions

Applicant FAQ

Do I need experience?

No. Most of our crew start with no restaurant experience. We train you station by station, and a Crew Trainer works alongside you during your first shifts.

How does the tuition help work?

Through Archways to Opportunity, team members employed for 90+ days who average 25+ hours a week can get up to $3,000 a year toward college, university or trade school, paid upfront to the school. Free high school diploma and English-language courses are also available.

How and when do I get paid?

Every other Tuesday by direct deposit. You can see your pay stubs and W-2 forms in the Mize Connect app.

Can I work around school or another job?

Yes. You set your availability in the LifeLenz app, and managers build schedules around it. Tell us your availability when you apply.

What happens after I apply?

A hiring manager from the restaurant you picked will contact you by phone or email to set up a short interview.

Ronald McDonald House Charities · Western Washington & Alaska

Keeping families together when it matters most.

The Seattle Ronald McDonald House is a home away from home for families from across Washington, Alaska and beyond whose children are being treated at Seattle Children's Hospital.

Visit rmhcseattle.org

What the House provides

  • Rooms for up to 105 families a night, near the hospital
  • Volunteer-cooked meals, stocked kitchens and laundry
  • Activities and play spaces for patients and siblings
  • A community of families going through the same thing

Our story

People first.
Standards always.

The Dhaliwal Organization runs three McDonald's restaurants in Kent and Puyallup, Washington, with more than 300 crew members, shift leaders and managers.

We believe great restaurants are built by taking care of the people who wear the headset, work the grill and greet every guest. When crew feel respected, trained with patience and supported in their goals, great service follows.

"People first, standards always" means we never compromise on either. We lead from the floor, promote from within, and hold ourselves to Quality, Service, Cleanliness and Value every hour.

Open portal

Can't make your shift? Call 3 hours ahead.

Call the restaurant at least 3 hours before your shift starts and speak with the manager on duty. Texts, LifeLenz messages and social media messages don't count as a call-out.

Your breaks (Washington State)

  • A paid 10-minute rest break for every 4 hours worked.
  • A 30-minute meal break on shifts over 5 hours, starting between your 2nd and 5th hour.
  • A free crew meal after every 5 hours worked.

Uniform & hygiene

  • Clean, wrinkle-free uniform and name tag
  • Non-slip shoes on every shift
  • Short nails, no polish or acrylics
  • Hair tied back under your hat or visor

First-day checklist

  • Original ID documents for Form I-9: one List A document, or one List B plus one List C document.
  • Your bank details for direct deposit
  • Mize Connect and LifeLenz set up on your phone

See the full list of acceptable I-9 documents

Food safety

Wash your hands every hour

Everyone on shift washes with warm water and soap for at least 20 seconds every hour, and every time you change stations or handle raw product.
